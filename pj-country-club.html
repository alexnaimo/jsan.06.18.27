Fine Print & Rules

Key clauses from the Lessing's contract — read before signing anything.

💰 Financial Fine Print

  • The 22% administrative fee is NOT gratuity — it does not go to the staff. You must tip separately (see Gratuity section above).
  • Credit cards are accepted for a maximum of $5,000 per transaction. Remaining balance must be paid by check or cash.
  • If additional labor is required (beyond the contracted staff), charges are $250/person per hour, plus the 22% admin fee and 8.75% sales tax.
  • Guest count is guaranteed at 150 minimum. You will be charged for at least 150 people regardless of actual attendance.
  • Final payment is due 14 days before the event — failure to pay may result in cancellation.

🚫 Prohibited Items & Violations

  • Throwing confetti, glitter, or rice is strictly prohibited. Violation results in a $2,000 cleanup fee charged to the client.
  • No pyrotechnics of any kind (sparklers, fireworks, smoke bombs, etc.).
  • No open flames other than those approved by the venue coordinator.
  • Client is responsible for any damage to the venue caused by guests or vendors.

📋 Outside Vendor Requirements

  • All outside vendors must carry $1,000,000 general liability insurance and name Lessing's as an additional insured.
  • Vendors must be submitted for approval at least 15 days before the event date.
  • Lessing's reserves the right to reject any vendor that does not meet their standards or insurance requirements.
  • Food and beverage service is exclusive to Lessing's — no outside food or drink vendors permitted.
  • Outside vendors must arrive, set up, and break down within the contracted venue access window.
