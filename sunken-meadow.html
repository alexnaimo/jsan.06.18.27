ℹ️ About This Venue

The Pavilion at Sunken Meadow is a state park venue on the Long Island Sound in Kings Park, NY. It offers stunning waterfront views and is a blank-canvas space — you bring your own caterer, bar service, rentals, and decor. Pricing details beyond $185/person are still TBD; update this planner as you get more info from the venue coordinator.

⚠️ State Park Rules to Confirm

  • NY State Parks permit is required for private events — confirm lead time and fees with the venue coordinator.
  • Alcohol permits may be required separately through NY State Parks.
  • Generator use may be required and/or restricted — confirm power access at the pavilion.
  • All vendors may need to be pre-approved by NY State Parks.
  • Amplified music and event end time may be regulated by park rules.
  • Restroom trailer may be required if the pavilion's facilities aren't sufficient for your headcount.
